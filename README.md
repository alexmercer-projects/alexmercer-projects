# Alex Mercer

I build small websites, spreadsheets and tools, scripts, and content. The work is practical and meant to be used.

The public repos under this account are concept demos and spec samples. The businesses in them are fictional. They show the kind of work I do, not case studies of paying clients.

## Websites

- [Juniper & Rye Bakehouse](https://github.com/alexmercer-projects/juniper-rye-bakehouse) — one-page site for a fictional neighborhood bakery. [Live demo](https://alexmercer-projects.github.io/juniper-rye-bakehouse/)
- [Hollis & Pike Home Repair](https://github.com/alexmercer-projects/hollis-pike-home-repair) — one-page site for a fictional handyman shop. [Live demo](https://alexmercer-projects.github.io/hollis-pike-home-repair/)
- [Wrenmill Community House](https://github.com/alexmercer-projects/wrenmill-community-house) — homepage for a fictional community hall. [Live demo](https://alexmercer-projects.github.io/wrenmill-community-house/)
- [Apparel shop layout mockup](https://github.com/alexmercer-projects/apparel-shop-layout-mockup) — a filterable product grid and product dialog with placeholder pieces. [Live demo](https://alexmercer-projects.github.io/apparel-shop-layout-mockup/)
- [Cedar & Clerk WordPress formatting](https://github.com/alexmercer-projects/cedar-clerk-wp-formatting) — an article laid out the way it might appear on WordPress. [Live demo](https://alexmercer-projects.github.io/cedar-clerk-wp-formatting/)
- [Ridgeline Plumbing local SEO](https://github.com/alexmercer-projects/ridgeline-plumbing-local-seo) — before and after on-page local SEO for a fictional plumber. [Live demo](https://alexmercer-projects.github.io/ridgeline-plumbing-local-seo/)
